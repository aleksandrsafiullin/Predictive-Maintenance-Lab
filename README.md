# Predictive Maintenance Lab

**Canonical version:** [`origin/main`](https://github.com/aleksandrsafiullin/Predictive-Maintenance-Lab). Other checkouts must be replaced with it before any work. Instructions for people and AI agents: [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

A local predictive-maintenance laboratory with independent projects and a Create ML-style workflow: **Projects → Import data → Data Quality → Training → Results → Live monitor**. Current long forecasts support **GRU, LSTM and MaleCNS**, use all available continuous received history after at least 60 observations, and preserve each saved model's full horizon. Calibration applies a bounded full corridor width, with default minimum 20% and maximum 45%. Measurements outside the corridor remain errors. Previous trend-corridor, RED-entry v2 and RUL implementations remain available for historical research and saved runs.

Предыдущий контракт обучения задавал коридор тренда с полушириной ±10% и обучаемым расширением до ±15%; полная ширина составляет 20–30%. Новая модель учится на фактических будущих значениях сигнала, без сглаживания задним числом. Окно первого RED следует из пересечений двух границ и условно на попадании сигнала внутрь коридора; это не калиброванная вероятность времени события. Если вторая граница не пересекает порог, окно остаётся открытым. Узкий коридор сам по себе не доказывает качество: результаты первого GRU остаются неудовлетворительными. Сохранённые модели первого контракта сохраняют исходные ±5–7,5%. См. [контракт и проверку изменения](docs/trend_corridor_20261005_ru.md). Историческая методика отдельных RED-моделей описана в [отчёте RED-entry v2](docs/red_entry_v2_implementation_report_ru.md).

See the [project workflow guide](docs/create_ml_workflow.md) for folder formats, automatic/manual splits, model settings, playback and recoverable deletion. The public datasets and earlier research below remain available without copying their raw files into each project.

**Live monitor** is a shared page for every project. It reads incoming sensor CSV files, shows current zones and forecasts each machine with its saved model. Current GRU, LSTM and MaleCNS forecasts use all continuous received history and the same saved Calibration corridor as Results; Calibration defaults to 20–45% full width. A separate browser-driven demo feed progressively supplies recorded Testing/Validation measurements. See [Live monitor](docs/live_monitor.md) for formats, model compatibility and usage.

**Fly brain · Full MaleCNS** is available in Training alongside GRU, LSTM and Quantile boosting when the official local connection and annotation files are present. It computes every classified neuron using the public directed graph; only the numeric signal readout is trained. See [source, computation and restoration](docs/full_cns_signal.md).

|---|---|---|---|
| Bearings / XJTU-SY | [Dataset authors](https://biaowang.tech/xjtu-sy-bearing-datasets/) | Horizontal and vertical acceleration RMS from a 1.28-second vibration fragment recorded once per minute | 9 train / 3 validation / 3 test units |
| Filters / HSE | [Public dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prognosticshse/preventive-to-predicitve-maintenance) and [source paper](https://papers.phmsociety.org/index.php/ijphm/article/download/3087/1835) | Differential pressure in Pa, flow and dust feed; source `Time` is seconds, with 0.1-second CSV steps | Original 40 / 10 / 50; admission retains 39 / 10 / 50 units |

The RUL column's duration scale follows Time in the CSV, but its unit is not independently restated in the schema. See [data units and endpoints](docs/data_units_and_endpoints.md).

## Current sensor zones

The existing sensor-zone research utilities describe both datasets without an RUL model or monitoring bundle. The primary project workflow now trains a signal corridor and derives RED timing from it. Historical signal and event runs retain their saved outputs.

- **Bearings:** green is vibration near that bearing's initial baseline; yellow is a persistent rise; red is measured RMS at least 2× that baseline. These are provisional signal rules, not expert fault diagnoses. [Rule and limitations](docs/health_zones.md).
- **Filters:** green is usable pressure below a provisional 300 Pa band, yellow is 300–<600 Pa, red is at least 600 Pa, and gray means an unusable or incomplete measurement. Green means only “below this provisional band.” The source's observed-event convention is strictly >600 Pa, whereas the display boundary is ≥600 Pa. [Rule and limitations](docs/filter_sensor_zones.md).

A bearing GRU trained on labels generated by the current-signal rule scored **0.790 on validation and 0.956 on test** for unit-balanced balanced accuracy. That is good agreement with the weak rule, but the rule itself scores 1.000 by construction. It does **not** establish true fault detection or prediction of future entry into red. There are only three independent bearing trajectories in each split. The filter 300 Pa boundary is a provisional tradeoff based on four observed training events and one validation event, with no observed event in the 50 test prefixes. See the [benchmark and threshold review](docs/sensor_zone_benchmark_review.md) and [implementation report](docs/sensor_zones_implementation_report.md).

### Future-red forecast experiment

The separate fixed-horizon experiment predicts whether the saved sensor-zone rule first enters red in the next **1,800 s for bearings** or **20 s for filters**. It evaluates GRU, LSTM, Fly and degree-matched Random models against simple baselines; Full MaleCNS has a separate bearing run. Targets come from causal sensor-zone labels and preserve unknown labels for right-censored, poor-quality or gapped horizons. The experiment is exploratory: only **3 bearing test event units** are observed, and the filter test split has **0 observed red events**. These weak rule-derived labels and small event counts do not support a production claim. See the [future-red forecast report](docs/future_red_forecast_report.md) for protocol, results and limits.

Reproduce the eight-model sensor-only matrix with prepared local data and saved zone labels:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_future_red_matrix.py \
  --datasets bearings,filters \
  --architectures gru,lstm,fly,random \
  --seed 42 --history-length 20 --epochs 30 --batch-size 64 \
  --hidden-size 64 --num-layers 1 --patience 5 --fly-nodes 1000 \
  --output-dir runs/_future_red/matrix/sensor_only_20260928_reproduction
```

The existing **0.790 / 0.956** scores above belong to the current-zone weak-label GRU benchmark. They measure agreement with the sensor rule that created the labels; they are not future-red forecast scores. See the [benchmark review](docs/sensor_zone_benchmark_review.md).

Prior remaining-useful-life (RUL) training artifacts and dependent monitoring bundles were removed from active `runs/` on 28 September 2026 and placed in a recoverable local Trash archive. Their published reports and legacy CLI remain as historical research; they are **not** current red-zone models. The future-red experiment above uses its own horizon-based target and unit-level evaluation.

English UI (Streamlit). Heavy jobs run in a background worker. Repository: [Predictive-Maintenance-Lab](https://github.com/aleksandrsafiullin/Predictive-Maintenance-Lab).

## What you need

- macOS, Linux, or Windows 10/11
- Python **3.11 or 3.12**
- PyTorch: **CPU** everywhere; **CUDA** on NVIDIA Windows/Linux; **MPS** only on Apple
- ~**8+ GB** free disk for filters + prepared data; bearings zip alone is ~**5 GB**
- Optional: Kaggle credentials for filters download (`kagglehub`)
- Optional: ~**1 GB** MaleCNS feather for real fly connectome (not required for GRU/LSTM)

`setup.sh` / `run.sh` are tested on **macOS arm64**. The `.ps1` scripts are written for Windows PowerShell; they are not executed on the macOS development machine.

Do **not** commit `data/`, `runs/`, or `.venv/`.

## 2. Download source data

### Filters (Kaggle)

Needs network access. First time, configure Kaggle for `kagglehub` (API token): https://www.kaggle.com/docs/api

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pdm download --dataset filters
```

Or copy a local extract:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pdm download --dataset filters --local-path /path/to/filters_folder
```

Dataset: [prognosticshse/preventive-to-predicitve-maintenance](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prognosticshse/preventive-to-predicitve-maintenance) (slug typo `predicitve` is intentional).

### Bearings (XJTU-SY, ~5 GB)

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pdm download --dataset bearings
```

Falls back to a public Hugging Face mirror of the author zip if needed. Author page: https://biaowang.tech/xjtu-sy-bearing-datasets/

Or pass a local zip/folder:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pdm download --dataset bearings --local-path /path/to/XJTU-SY_Bearing_Datasets.zip
```

### Both

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pdm download --dataset all
```

Raw files land under `data/raw/{filters,bearings}/`.

## 4. Build and inspect current zones

After preparing both datasets, export versioned sensor labels and optionally train the bearing model that reproduces its weak signal rule:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pdm zones-labels --dataset bearings
.venv/bin/python -m pdm zones-labels --dataset filters
.venv/bin/python -m pdm zones-train
```

The filter zone screen uses the exported labels and prepared measurements directly; no RUL training is required. `zones-train` currently trains **bearings only** and classifies the present zone. It does not predict future entry into red. Versioned outputs are saved under `runs/_zones/`.

Optional synthetic connectome smoke demo (a test fixture, not a quality result and not a biological connectome):

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pdm train --dataset bearings --arch fly_connectome_reservoir --smoke --n-nodes 8
```

Launch the UI:

```bash
./scripts/run.sh
# or: .venv/bin/python -m pdm app
```

Open **http://127.0.0.1:8501**. In **Projects**, enter a name and source format, then choose **Create project**. **Import data** accepts a browser-selected folder or a folder path visible to the app's computer. Generic CSV files require `unit_id`, `timestamp_s` and a chosen numeric signal. Use a server folder path for multi-GB sources such as XJTU-SY.

**Import data** has Training Data, Validation Data and Testing Data cards. Validation and Testing each offer **Split from training** or **Separate folder**; **Split settings** controls weights and seed. Saved counts and **View** link each card to its Data Quality tab. HSE's official Test file can be supplied in the Training source or in a separate Testing folder; its histories always remain held out. See the [workflow guide](docs/create_ml_workflow.md).

**Data Quality** shows the three sets, their units, measurements, gaps and signal zones. **Move units** changes the allocation of whole histories and creates a new snapshot requiring new training. Yellow/red limits are edited here after import: edits preview the zones, **Save** persists them for Results, and **Cancel** restores the saved rule. Saving limits preserves the trained model; a first Sensor CSV import has no zone rule until one is provided.

**Training** offers one Trend corridor task and four engines. **Results** shows the learned boundaries, derived RED window, measured containment and width. Old runs retain their original objective and are labeled accordingly. Play/Pause/Reset and the observation slider control the replay cursor; future measurements are review-only. See the [corridor contract](docs/trend_corridor_20261005_ru.md) and [workflow guide](docs/create_ml_workflow.md).

To check the repository:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests -q
.venv/bin/python -m ruff check src tests scripts
```

## Important caveats

- `--smoke` is **not** a quality benchmark.  
- Healthy regime references and statistical thresholds are provisional. Missing reference, uncalibrated bands and unavailable action timing must not be interpreted as safe operation.
- Filter source `Time` uses seconds (`time_to_seconds=1`), supported by HSE Figure 6. The RUL unit is inferred from the CSV relationship and action timing is not validated for field use.
- `filters_full_history_v1` remains disabled pending verified MATLAB table export, origin matching and endpoint/unit checks; an export helper is not evidence of a completed import.
- Connectome Explorer shows **computational** reservoir activity, not a biophysical fly-brain recording.  
- Do not compare models across incompatible dataset versions / gap-rule versions / splits.
